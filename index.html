What this site collects

Nothing directly. This site has no forms, accounts, cookies of its own or advertising, and it does not currently use analytics. Its pages, fonts and images are served from our own site.

Hosting and server logs

This site is delivered through Cloudflare, which acts as our hosting and security provider. Cloudflare processes visitors' IP addresses and request details, such as browser type and pages requested, to deliver the site, block attacks and keep standard logs. It may also set strictly necessary security cookies. We do not use this data to identify visitors. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

When you email us

The contact buttons open your email app. If you send us a message, we receive your email address and whatever you include. We use it only to reply and to scope or deliver work you ask about. We do not sell or share it for marketing, and we share it with service providers only as needed to operate our email and business systems.

Retention and your requests

We keep correspondence only as long as needed for the purpose above or as required by contract or law. To ask what we hold about you, or to have it corrected or deleted, email us and we will respond promptly.

Changes

If this changes, for example if we add analytics, we will update this page and its date before the change takes effect.
